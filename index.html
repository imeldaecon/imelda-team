On-going Projects

1
Surviving with Interruptions: Firm Adaptation to Electricity Rationing 🇿🇦 South Africa

Estimates how firms across sectors adjust production, and investment decisions in response to scheduled electricity rationing.

2
Crime in the Dark: Role of Electricity Rationing 🇿🇦 South Africa

Examines how planned electricity outages alter criminal activity by reducing lighting, disrupting surveillance, and shifting the geography of crime.

3
Coal Mining & Electricity Shortage 🇿🇦 South Africa

Traces how rainfall shocks flood mines and disrupt coal deliveries, reducing power plant availability and triggering load shedding — revealing how climate variability already threatens grid reliability today.

4
Smart Metering and Payment Compliance 🇮🇩 Indonesia

Studies how Indonesia's large-scale smart metering rollout affects payment compliance, consumption responses, and whether efficiency gains are distributed equitably across income groups.
